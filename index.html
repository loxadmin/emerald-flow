Karbali Ads — Find Customers and Get Paying Clients Online

Karbali Ads is a performance advertising platform that helps brands, businesses, apps, and shops find real customers and paying clients. You only pay for measurable results — signups, downloads, conversions, and sales — never for empty impressions or wasted clicks.

Why advertisers choose Karbali Ads

  • Pay only for verified results: signups, app installs, leads, and purchases.
  • Built-in conversion tracking, attribution, and fraud protection.
  • Engagement, performance, and commerce ad formats in one platform.
  • Transparent escrow billing — your budget is only deducted on real outcomes.
  • Real-time analytics, campaign control, and instant top-ups from your wallet.

Ad types built for real growth

Run engagement ads to grow brand awareness with quizzes and rewarded media, performance ads to acquire users and leads at a fixed cost per action, and commerce ads to drive verified online sales with commission-based pricing.

Who Karbali Ads is for

Startups looking for early users, mobile apps that need installs, e-commerce stores that want paying customers, SaaS businesses driving signups, and established brands that want measurable customer acquisition instead of guesswork.

How it works

  1. Create your advertiser account and verify your business email.
  2. Fund your wallet and launch a campaign — engagement, performance, or commerce.
  3. Install the Karbali tracker or share your tracking link to start measuring conversions.
  4. Pay only when real users complete the goals you defined.
